DAILY MEDITATIONS — 366 MOMENTS OF REFLECTION

Deploy: upload the contents of this ZIP (not the enclosing folder) to the root of a GitHub repository. Enable Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root).

Features: 366 statements, twelve themes, keyword search, stable year-seeded daily order, random discovery, favorites, share, quiet mode, backup/restore, installable PWA, offline assets.

IMPORTANT EDITORIAL NOTES:
- All 366 statements are newly drafted for this build, including the 24 prototype statements. No external quotations are included yet. The requested 75/25 blend remains an editorial phase requiring verified source texts and attributions.
- The 366 JPEG files are different photographic crops/color compositions derived from FOUR visual source images; they are not 366 independent nature photographs. This is an interim visual library, not the final unique-photograph collection.
- PWA installation and service worker offline caching require HTTPS hosting (GitHub Pages works) or localhost. The initial cache download is approximately 45 MB and may take time. Wait for it to finish before going offline.
- In a 365-day year one meditation is accessible by browsing/random rather than a calendar day. On leap years all 366 are scheduled.
- No accounts, remote analytics, or personal-data transmission are built in.
